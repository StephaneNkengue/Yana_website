Subscription

What's free, and what does Premium add?

Without a subscription, Yana stays open with 3 topics (Confidence, Stress & anxiety, Hard times), today's question, widgets, reminders, your favorites, your own lines, plain themes and two photo themes.

Yana Premium opens all 31 topics, the selections (Affirmations, Short quotes, Great authors), every theme and your own photo as a backdrop. Anything reserved shows a “Premium” badge.

How does the free trial work?

The monthly and yearly plans both start with a 7-day free trial, handled by the App Store. Yana reminds you the day before the trial ends. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the subscription starts automatically.

The trial is available once per Apple ID. If the paywall doesn't show it, your Apple account has already used it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. From Yana: Profile → Settings → Manage my subscription. Or from your iPhone:

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Yana.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep Premium until the end of the period already paid for, then Yana switches to the free version: nothing is erased.

I changed iPhones: how do I get my subscription back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Profile → Settings → Restore my purchases. The “Restore” button is also on the paywall.

How do I request a refund?

Payments are collected by Apple, which also handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account & data

Do I need an account?

No. Yana creates a guest account on your iPhone, with no email. To find your favorites, topics and own lines on another device, link it to Apple or Google: Profile → Settings → Protect your favorites.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. Your account, first name, favorites, topics and own lines are erased right away, for good. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your Apple subscription: cancel it first.

Can't open the app anymore? Write to customersupport.app@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Who can read what I write?

No one. Your own lines are kept for you, never read, never processed by AI. Your answer to today's question stays on your phone. It's all detailed in the privacy policy.

How do I change my first name or language?

In Profile → Settings: first name, reminders, hidden content and language (English or French). Quotes follow the app's language.

Quotes & topics

How does today's question work?

At the first opening of the day, Yana asks how you're arriving. Your feed starts with 8 lines from the topics that fit your answer, and one line in three in your widgets and reminders follows your mood until evening. To change your answer, tap the pill at the top of the feed. In the free version, only the moods tied to free topics change your feed.

How do I choose my topics?

Tap the topics icon at the bottom left of the feed, then Edit to follow or unfollow a topic. Tapping a topic opens it on its own.

How does my flame of the day light up?

Like (heart) 5 different lines in your feed. Each day your flame lights up counts toward your streak, and one missed day a week is forgiven. Removing a favorite doesn't take anything away.

I don't like a line. How do I hide it?

Touch and hold the line and tap “Not for me”: it won't come back. You'll find it in Profile → Settings → Hidden content if you change your mind.

How do I add my own lines?

On the topics screen, tap My own quotes. Add them one by one or import several, one per line. They join your feed, reminders and widgets.

How do I change the backdrop?

Tap the image icon at the bottom right of the feed. Pick a shelf (Sea & sky, Nature, Seasons, Cozy, Strength…), tap “Surprise me” for a random theme, or keep your favorites with a touch and hold. “My photo” sets a photo from your library as the backdrop.

Widgets & reminders

How do I add the widget?
  1. Touch and hold your Home Screen (or Lock Screen, then Customize).
  2. Tap + or Edit → Add Widget.
  3. Search for “Yana”, pick a size and tap Add Widget.

The guide is also in the app: Profile → Home Screen widgets → How to add the widget.

How do I set what the widget shows?

In Profile → Home Screen widgets or Lock Screen: the topic (your topics, one topic, your favorites, your own lines), the pace (from every hour to never), the backdrop and the font. To hide the buttons on a single widget: touch and hold it → Edit Widget.

My widget doesn't change lines.

Open Yana once: the app refreshes what the widget reads. Also check its pace in Profile → Home Screen widgets → Refresh. iOS may delay updates a little when the battery is low.

I'm not getting my reminders.
  1. Open Settings → Notifications → Yana and turn on “Allow Notifications”.
  2. Make sure a Focus mode isn't silencing Yana.
  3. In Yana, open Profile → Reminders: how many a day, start time and end time.
