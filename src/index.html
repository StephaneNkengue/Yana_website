How it works

One goal. Your words. A path.

No library of ready-made affirmations. Yana starts from what you actually want, and writes it for you, every day.

01

Say what you want

Write or dictate your goal in your own words. A few gentle questions help Yana understand where you're starting from and what's holding you back.

“I want to dare to say no at work.”
02

Yana maps your journey

It suggests a length — 14 to 90 days — and tells you why. You confirm, and your four-phase plan is built, with a clear finish line.

03

Listen, morning and evening

Each session is written for that day: your day in the journey, your mood, what you did yesterday and how it went. It adjusts after every listen.

The daily ritual

Two moments that don't say the same thing.

The morning commits. The evening looks back, and closes the loop on the morning's action.

Morning

Your reason to move today, and one small mission.

  1. 1Check-in: how you feel
  2. 2Grounding: breathe, come back here

  4. 4Affirmations written for you

What you did with your mission. If you didn't do it, no blame.

  1. 1Check-in: mission done? any proof?
  2. 2A look back at your day

  4. 4Letting go, to sleep well

Simple enough to become a habit. Personal enough to matter.

The journey

A path in four phases, at your pace.

Each phase covers a quarter of your journey, whatever its length. Sessions change tone as you go: first believe it, then live it, then do it.

14 days21 days30 days60 days90 days

Yana recommends a length based on your goal. You can extend it later.

1

Believe0–25 %

Make your goal believable.

2

Become25–50 %

Embody it, speak of it in the present.

3

Act50–75 %

Take action, a slightly bigger step each day.

4

Anchor75–100 %

Make it stick, and prepare what comes next.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

What does “manifestation” mean with Yana?

Picturing the life you want clearly, believing in it, then acting toward it every day. Yana combines visualization, affirmations written for you, and one small concrete mission each morning. Visualization alone isn't enough — the daily action is what counts.

What if I miss a day?

Your journey simply pauses. It doesn't skip a day: you pick up where you left off, with no blame.

Does Yana use artificial intelligence?

Yes. Your sessions are written and voiced by AI models, based on your goal and feedback. The app asks for your consent before sending anything, and explains what is shared and with whom.

Is my data private?

Nothing you write is ever made public or sold. You can delete your account and all your data from Profile → Delete my account. Details in the privacy policy.

Is this therapy?

No. Yana is a personal growth tool, not a medical or psychological service. If you are going through a hard time, please talk to a health professional.

Is Yana available on Android?

Not yet. Yana is coming to iPhone first (iOS 17 or later).
