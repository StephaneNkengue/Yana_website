FAQ

Your questions, answered.

What is Yana?

An iPhone app for daily affirmations and quotes. You follow topics, scroll lines full screen, and find them in your widgets and reminders. Each day, a question about your mood opens your feed on the lines you need.

How does today's question work?

At the first opening of the day, Yana asks how you're arriving, with six answers. Your feed starts with 8 lines from the matching topics, and one line in three in your widgets and reminders follows your mood until evening. You can skip the question, or change your answer from the pill at the top of the feed.

Does Yana use artificial intelligence?

No. No AI runs in the app and no text is generated for you: the catalog is written ahead of time, in French and English, and reviewed before it's published. Yana only sorts: your topics, your mood, the time of day, what you've already seen.

Is Yana free?

Yes, with 3 topics, today's question, widgets, reminders, your favorites, your own lines and plain themes. Yana Premium opens all 31 topics and every theme, with a 7-day free trial on both the monthly and yearly plans.

Do I need an account?

No. Yana works right away, with no sign-up. If you want to find your favorites on another iPhone, you can link your account to Apple or Google in the settings.

Is my data private?

Yes. No ads, no tracking, nothing is sold. Your answer to today's question never leaves your phone. You can delete your account and data from Profile → Settings → Delete my account. Details in the privacy policy.

Is this therapy?

No. Yana is an everyday well-being app, not a medical or psychological service. If you're going through a hard time, please talk to a health professional.

Is Yana available on Android?

Not yet. Yana is coming to iPhone first (iOS 17 or later).
